Privacy Policy

Last updated: August 14, 2026

This website (mrtaihualin.com) is operated by Tai Hua Thai Language Teaching. We value your personal data and privacy. This policy explains how we collect, use, and protect information.

1. Information we collect

Depending on the services you use, we may collect the following information:

Class and contact information

When you book a class, submit a form, or contact us, we may collect the name, email address, contact details, and message you provide to arrange classes, reply to you, and provide related services.

Game account information

If you register or sign in to a game account, you may use an email verification code, Google, Facebook, or LINE.

When you use Google or Facebook to sign in, we receive basic account information such as your name, email address, and profile picture according to your authorization and the information the platform actually provides.

Because LINE does not always provide a real email address, the system may create a virtual email address used only to identify your account internally (for example, line-[identifier]@users.line.invalid). It is not your real email address and will not be used to send email.

Game and learning information

When you use a game account, we may record account-related learning progress, game results, scores, saved content, and other data needed to provide learning records and account features.

Security and system records

To protect accounts and the website and to prevent cheating, abuse, or unusual activity, we may record IP addresses, device-related information, sign-in records, and necessary security records.

If you link or unlink an account or perform another important account action, the system may record the time and relevant details to confirm account status, investigate unusual activity, and support necessary recovery.

2. How we use your information

We may use the information described above to:

  • Provide and manage Thai classes and related services
  • Create and manage game accounts
  • Save learning progress, game results, and user settings
  • Provide sign-in, account verification, and account security features
  • Prevent cheating, abuse, unauthorized access, and other unusual activity
  • Reply to questions and handle account issues
  • Analyze overall website and game usage to improve the services
  • Meet applicable legal obligations

We do not sell your personal data.

3. Cookies and analytics tools

We may use cookies, local storage, or similar technologies to provide sign-in, account security, and basic website functions.

Technologies that are necessary for the website to work may be used to the extent required for those functions.

We also use the following analytics tools:

  • Google Analytics 4 (GA4): Helps us understand overall website and game browsing, clicks, and other usage.
  • Microsoft Clarity: Helps us understand page interactions such as clicks, scrolling, heatmaps, and user flows so we can improve the experience.

Analytics cookies and similar technologies are configured according to your cookie choice. Microsoft Clarity uses analytics cookies only after you consent.

You may accept or reject analytics cookies and later change your choice through the website's “Cookie Settings”.

If you reject, Microsoft Clarity will not use analytics cookies, but limited cookieless tracking or usage measurement may still occur under Consent API V2.

Rejecting analytics cookies does not affect the website's or games' basic functions.

4. Third-party services and data processing

To provide website and game features, we may use third-party service providers, including:

  • Google
  • Meta (Facebook)
  • LINE
  • Microsoft (Clarity)
  • Providers of website, database, authentication, and other necessary technical services

These providers may process relevant information to the extent necessary to provide sign-in verification, website analytics, system operations, or security protection.

Some providers may process or store information outside Taiwan. Where cross-border data transfers occur, we will handle them according to applicable laws and data protection requirements.

5. Data retention

We retain personal data only for as long as necessary to provide services, protect security, handle disputes, meet legal obligations, or serve another legitimate and necessary purpose.

Retention periods may differ by data type. For example:

  • Account and learning data may be retained while the account remains active.
  • Security and operation records may be retained for as long as needed to prevent abuse, investigate unusual activity, or protect system security.
  • Data generated by third-party analytics tools is handled according to each service's actual settings and retention mechanisms.

When information no longer needs to be retained, we will delete it, anonymize it, or handle it in another appropriate way, unless retention is required by law or is necessary for security or dispute handling.

6. Your rights

Depending on applicable data protection laws and your circumstances, you may have the right to:

  • Ask about or request access to your personal data
  • Request correction of inaccurate or incomplete data
  • Request deletion or anonymization of your personal data
  • Request restriction of certain processing
  • Object to certain processing
  • Request a copy or transfer of your data where applicable
  • Withdraw consent you previously provided

You may also request deletion of your game account. Some data that must be temporarily retained for legal obligations, security protection, dispute handling, or system backups may not be deleted immediately.

To exercise these rights or ask about personal data, please use the contact information in this policy.

7. Data security

We take reasonable technical and administrative measures to protect your personal data and reduce the risk of unauthorized access, alteration, disclosure, loss, or misuse.

However, no method of internet transmission or electronic storage can be guaranteed to be completely secure.

8. Changes to this Privacy Policy

We may update this policy when website features, services, technologies, or legal requirements change.

The updated version will be posted on this page with a revised “Last updated” date. If a change materially affects your rights or how information is processed, we will provide appropriate notice where practical.
